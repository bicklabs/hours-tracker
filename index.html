Privacy

  • Your entries, locations and settings are saved only in this app on this phone.
  • Nothing is sent to any server. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.
  • Anyone else using the app has their own separate data. No one can see yours.
  • Deleting the app from your home screen erases its data, so back up regularly.
